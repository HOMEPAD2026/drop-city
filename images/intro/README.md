# Intro fly-through art (`images/intro/`)

Vintage public-domain artwork for the ~12 s fly-through intro: six back tableaux (`s1`…`s6`) and five foreground cut-outs (`f2`…`f6`). **Not generated:** every image is a crop of a public-domain or CC0 work, or a cut-out made from one. The image generators (OpenAI and Meshy) were out of credits, so nothing here comes from a model.

The source originals are in `promo/intro/raw/*-src.jpg`. Each licence below was checked on the source page on 2026-11-20.
