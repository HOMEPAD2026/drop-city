FEES

pump.fun pays every coin's creator a share of its trading fees. For every coin launched on the platform that share is split three ways — locked on chain forever when the coin launches. (The main coin is the dev's own: its creator fees go 100% to the dev.)

No launch fee from us — you pay about 0.03 SOL of Solana network and rent costs, plus any first buy you choose. pump.fun's own trading fees apply as on pump.fun.

ABOUT

What is this?

A launchpad that turns pump.fun launches into a city. The city's own agent drops one coin at the top of every hour, and anyone can launch their own coins any time.

Who holds my coin or my keys?

Nobody but you. You launch from your own wallet; the coin's address key is made in your browser and used once to create the coin. Our server hosts the logo and details and checks the chain.

What does THE VAULT do?

It collects 30 % of the creator fees of every coin launched on the platform and uses them to buy back and burn the coins at the top of MOST WANTED. Every move is a public transaction.

What is the 10 %?

It goes to a separate wallet that buys the city's main coin.

Does the main coin split its fees too?

No. The main coin is the dev's own coin, launched before the platform: its creator fees go 100% to the dev. The 60 / 30 / 10 split is for the coins launched on the platform — the agent's hourly drops and everyone's launches.

What is the hourly drop?

An agent that runs the city launches one coin at the top of every UTC hour from its own wallet, with the same locked fee split. Its 60 % creator share goes on to THE VAULT (the agent keeps a small reserve to pay for its next launches). Its names and art are original.

Is there a limit for me?

No. Launch as many coins as you like, whenever you like.

Is this financial advice?

No. Meme coins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Only use money you can lose.
